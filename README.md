# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes 3,900 customer purchases to uncover spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behavior.

The project uses Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to transform transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- PostgreSQL: Data analysis and business queries
- Power BI: Interactive dashboard and data visualization

## Dataset

- 3,900 purchase records
- 18 columns
- Customer demographics, purchase details, and shopping behavior
- 37 missing values in the Review Rating column

## Project Workflow

1. Data cleaning and preparation using Python.
2. Exploratory data analysis and feature engineering.
3. Integration of the cleaned dataset with PostgreSQL.
4. SQL analysis to answer business questions.
5. Interactive dashboard development in Power BI.
6. Business recommendations based on analytical findings.

## Key Business Questions

- How does revenue differ by gender and age group?
- Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
- Which products have the highest ratings and purchase counts?
- How do discounts influence customer purchases?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
- How do Standard and Express shipping customers compare?

## Business Recommendations

- Promote subscription benefits to encourage customer sign-ups.
- Introduce loyalty programs for repeat buyers.
- Review discount strategies to balance sales and profitability.
- Highlight highly rated and best-selling products.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue customer segments.
